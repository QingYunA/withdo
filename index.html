How a day goes

One click

First, open Settings › Agents and click your agent. Withdo adds itself as its MCP server.

All day, on its own

Then the agent reports as it works: the repo, the branch, the step it is on, the pull request it opened.

One sentence

When it is stuck, its card turns red. You answer and it carries on.

One look

Last, its pull request waits for your review.

Opening the first time

The Mac and Windows builds are not signed yet. On a Mac, open Withdo once, then choose Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway. Updates after that install by themselves.

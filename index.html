Withdo

Your tasks and your agents' work, in one place.

Withdo is a todo app for you and your coding agents. Type or speak a sentence and it turns into tasks with their days and times. Hand a task to Claude Code, Codex or another agent and watch what it is doing from the same place.

  • One input: dates, reminders and categories read from what you write.
  • Voice input that fills the box, so you can fix a word first.
  • A board of what your coding agents (Claude Code, Codex and others) are working on.
  • Your tasks sync between your Mac, Windows PC and Android phone.
  • Open source under the AGPL-3.0: github.com/QingYunA/withdo.

Download

macOS Windows Android

Apple silicon and Intel Macs, Windows x64, Android 8 and newer. The app updates itself after that. All files

The Mac and Windows apps are not signed yet. On a Mac, open Withdo once, then choose Open Anyway in System Settings > Privacy & Security. On Windows, choose More info > Run anyway.

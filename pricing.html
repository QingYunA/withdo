How billing works

  • Upgrade from Settings › Account in the app. Checkout opens in your browser.
  • Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are processed by Waffo Pancake, our merchant of record, which also collects and remits sales tax and VAT. PayPal payments are processed by PayPal.
  • The trial costs nothing. If you cancel before it ends, you are not charged. Otherwise the first charge is on the day the trial ends.
  • Cancel any time from Settings › Account › Manage subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • Refunds follow section 7 of the Terms.
